    1. What are cookies?

    Cookies are small text files that are placed on your computer or mobile device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently, to remember your preferences, and to provide information to the site owners.

    Cookies can be "persistent" (remaining on your device until they expire or you delete them) or "session" cookies (deleted when you close your browser). They can also be "first-party" (set by us) or "third-party" (set by another domain).

    2. How we use cookies

    Interior Lines Journal uses cookies for a limited number of purposes:

    • To ensure our website functions correctly and securely.
    • To remember your cookie consent preferences.
    • To understand how readers use our site so we can improve content and navigation.
    • To remember display settings, such as font size or layout preferences.

    We do not use cookies to serve targeted advertising or to build advertising profiles of our readers.

    3. Types of cookies we use

    The table below describes the specific cookies we set and their purpose.

    Cookie name Type Purpose Duration
    ilj_consent Essential Stores your cookie consent preferences so we don't ask on every visit. 12 months
    ilj_session Essential Maintains your session while browsing and supports basic security. Session
    ilj_prefs Preference Remembers display preferences such as dark mode or font size. 6 months
    _ga, _gid Analytics Google Analytics — helps us understand traffic patterns and popular articles (anonymised where possible). 2 years / 24 hours

    We keep our use of analytics cookies to a minimum and configure them to anonymise IP addresses where the provider supports it.

    4. Third-party cookies

    Some cookies on our site are set by trusted third-party services that help us operate and improve our website. These include:

    • Google Analytics — for anonymous, aggregate traffic analysis.
    • Google Fonts — to deliver typography; may log requests for performance and abuse prevention.
    • Email service providers — if you subscribe to our newsletter, the provider may set cookies when you interact with emails or forms.

    These third parties have their own privacy and cookie policies. We do not control the cookies they set, but we only work with providers who respect user privacy.

    5. Managing cookies

    You have several options for controlling or disabling cookies:

    • Browser settings — most browsers allow you to block or delete cookies. The "Help" section of your browser will show you how.
    • Our consent banner — when you first visit, you can accept or decline non-essential cookies. You can change your choice at any time via the "Cookie Settings" link in the footer.
    • Opt-out tools — for analytics, you can install the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

    Please note that disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality of our site. Essential cookies cannot be disabled through our consent tool as they are required for the site to work.

    Do Not Track

    Some browsers offer a "Do Not Track" (DNT) signal. Our site does not currently respond to DNT signals, but we do not engage in cross-site tracking for advertising purposes. You can manage tracking through the cookie controls described above.

    6. Changes to this policy

    We may update this Cookies Policy from time to time to reflect changes in technology, regulation, or our practices. When we make changes, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

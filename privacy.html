    1. Information we collect

    We collect information you voluntarily provide to us, as well as certain data automatically when you browse our site.

    Information you provide

    • Contact details — name, email address, and any message content when you use our contact form or email us directly.
    • Newsletter subscription — email address if you choose to subscribe to our editorial newsletter.
    • Pitches & submissions — any materials, links, or personal information you share when submitting story ideas.

    Information collected automatically

    • Usage data — pages visited, time spent, referring URLs, and general interaction with our content.
    • Device & browser data — IP address, browser type, operating system, and screen resolution.
    • Cookies & similar technologies — small text files that help us remember preferences and understand site performance.

    2. How we use your information

    We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

    • To respond to your inquiries, corrections, and story pitches.
    • To send our newsletter if you have opted in (you can unsubscribe at any time).
    • To improve our website, content, and reader experience.
    • To monitor site security and prevent abuse.
    • To comply with legal obligations.

    We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

    3. Cookies & tracking

    We use minimal cookies to ensure our site functions properly and to understand how readers engage with our articles. These include:

    • Essential cookies — required for basic site functionality (e.g., remembering your cookie preferences).
    • Analytics cookies — help us understand traffic patterns and popular content. We use privacy-focused analytics where possible.
    • Preference cookies — remember settings like your display preferences.

    You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect some site features. For more details, see our Cookies Policy.

    4. Third-party services

    We may use trusted third-party services to support our operations, including:

    • Email delivery providers — to send newsletters and respond to inquiries.
    • Analytics platforms — to understand site usage in aggregate.
    • Font & asset delivery — Google Fonts and similar services for typography and images.

    These providers have their own privacy policies and are only given access to the information necessary to perform their specific functions. We do not share personal data with advertisers or data brokers.

    5. Your rights

    Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal information:

    • Access — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
    • Correction — ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete information.
    • Deletion — request that we delete your personal data, subject to legal retention requirements.
    • Opt-out — unsubscribe from marketing communications at any time.
    • Restriction — request that we limit how we process your data.

    To exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@interiorlinesjournal.com. We will respond within the timeframe required by applicable law.

    6. Data security

    We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. These include encrypted connections (HTTPS), access controls, and regular reviews of our data practices.

    However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your data, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

    7. Data retention

    We retain personal information only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy, unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by law. Contact form submissions are typically retained for 12 months, and newsletter subscriptions are retained until you unsubscribe.

    8. Children's privacy

    Our content is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will take steps to delete it.

    9. International transfers

    Our website is operated from the United States. If you access our site from outside the U.S., your information may be transferred to, stored, and processed in the U.S. By using our site, you consent to this transfer.

    10. Changes to this policy

    We may update this privacy policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this page periodically to stay informed about how we protect your information.

    11. Contact us

    If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this privacy policy or your personal data, please reach out:

    • Email: privacy@interiorlinesjournal.com
    • Mail: Interior Lines Journal, Editorial Office, 1420 SE Morrison St, Suite 200, Portland, OR 97214

    This privacy policy is provided for informational purposes and does not constitute legal advice. For specific legal concerns, please consult a qualified attorney.

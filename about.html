    About the Journal

    Independent editorial for people who care about how spaces feel.

    Interior Lines Journal is a publication devoted to residential architecture and interior design — long-form articles, practical guides, and critical perspective on the built environment.

    Our Mission

    We believe that good design is a form of care — for the people who live in a space, for the materials we use, and for the long life of a building. Our work is guided by a simple question: how do design decisions affect the way people actually live?

    Interior Lines Journal exists to answer that question with clarity, depth, and independence. We are not a design firm, a product marketplace, or a portfolio. We are a publication. That distinction matters: it allows us to write honestly about what works, what doesn't, and why — without commercial obligation.

    “We prioritize clarity over jargon, evidence over trends, and usefulness over aspiration alone.” — Elena Marsh, Editor-in-Chief

    Editorial Approach

    Every article is written with the reader in mind. Whether you are a design professional seeking a deeper perspective, a homeowner planning a renovation, or simply someone who cares about the quality of everyday spaces, we aim to meet you where you are.

    • Depth over speed. We take the time to report thoroughly and think carefully before publishing.
    • Practical usefulness. Theory matters, but so does knowing which countertop will stain and which lighting layout will actually work.
    • Material honesty. We write about finishes, fixtures, and construction methods with the specificity they deserve.
    • No hidden agendas. We do not accept sponsored content or paid placements. Our recommendations are our own.

    What We Cover

    Our editorial spans the full breadth of residential design — from the structural logic of a well-planned home to the tactile qualities of a hand-thrown ceramic tile. Regular sections include:

    • Architecture — the form, proportion, and spatial logic of residential buildings
    • Interior Design — color, texture, furniture, and the composition of interior space
    • Materials — stone, timber, tile, metal, and the practicalities of specification
    • Lighting — both natural and artificial, from daylighting strategy to fixture selection
    • Space Planning — how to make rooms work harder and feel better
    • Home Design — kitchens, baths, home offices, and the details that shape daily life

    Independence & Transparency

    Interior Lines Journal is independently owned and operated. We do not run display advertising, sponsored posts, or affiliate links. Our revenue comes from reader subscriptions to our newsletter, The Weekly Line, and from occasional grants that support long-form reporting.

    We believe that an editorial voice is only as trustworthy as its independence. If we ever change our funding model, we will say so clearly and prominently.
